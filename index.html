🔊 Physical Acoustic Principle
A controlled haptic excitation sweep (80–1,800 Hz) vibrates the glass wall into the liquid column. Pure milk solids and emulsified fats create distinct viscous damping (ζ ≈ 0.043) and sound speed (c ≈ 1,532 m/s). Dilution with water shifts the resonant frequency downward and lowers the damping envelope.
Physical Positioning
Place Phone Against Glass
Firm mechanical coupling transmits vibration and listens to resonance
Coupling Protocol Checklist
1
Liquid Level: Ensure the glass is at least half full (100–200 mL) for adequate liquid damping.
2
Direct Contact: Hold the phone vertically with its lower-middle back firmly against the outer glass wall.
3
Zero Air Gap: Maintain steady pressure. Avoid loose cases or sleeves that disrupt acoustic transmission.
4
Keep Steady: Hold still during the excitation pulse; do not tap or slide the device.
